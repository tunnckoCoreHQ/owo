# The Blue Glaze

## What is open

I want a glaze the colour of the harbor under the ferry ramp on a still morning. That is a deep blue with grey in it, not the bright blue of a postcard. I want it for a set of mugs for the bakery, and for myself. I started on 20 October with a satin base that [[Ines]] uses, plus a little cobalt carbonate and rutile, on our usual stoneware body.

Round one came out grey-green everywhere. Ines thinks the iron in the clay body comes through where the glaze is thin. For round two I added more cobalt and changed the base at the same time. Those tiles came out flat navy and dead, and I could not tell which change had done it, so I learned nothing from a whole firing.

For round three, on 28 October, I went back to the first base and changed only the cobalt, at three strengths on carved tiles. They went into the kiln load of 2 November. The middle strength is close. It has the right blue where the glaze pools in the carved lines, but the raised edges are still grey-green.

Next I fire the middle recipe again, unchanged, with a slow cool that Ines is writing into the kiln program. If the edges still go green, the next single change is a thicker coat. I change one thing per round, and I label every tile on the back in pencil before it goes in.

This closes when I have one tile that is the harbor colour on both the pooled parts and the edges, and the same result twice.
