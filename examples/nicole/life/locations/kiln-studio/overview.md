# Kiln Studio

## Description

The Kiln Studio is an old net store on the harbor road, one long room with a tin roof and whitewashed walls that have gone grey at hand height. Two wheels stand by the door, where the floor drains. The electric kiln sits in a brick bay at the back with its own fan, and a long table runs under the north window. My drawing desk is at the far end of that table, where the light is steadiest.

It smells of wet clay, coffee and, on firing days, a faint hot-metal smell from the kiln bay. When it rains the roof is so loud that we stop talking. From the window I can see the ferry ramp and the corner of the bakery.

## Key facts

- One electric kiln, shared. [[Ines]] keeps the firing schedule on a chalkboard by the bay.
- The bisque shelf by the kiln is where pots wait to dry. Nothing leaves that shelf until it passes the cheek test.
- The radiator is weak, so in the colder months washes and greenware both dry slowly.
- The windowsill above my desk holds the test tiles for [[The Blue Glaze]].

## Relationships

- I share it with [[Ines]], who has worked from here for years.
- It is where the [[First Firing]] happened, and where the [[Studio Year]] started.
