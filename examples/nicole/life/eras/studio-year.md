# Studio Year

## Description

The Studio Year started on 1 September, the day I carried my drawing desk down the harbor road and set it under the north window of the [[Kiln Studio]]. Before that I drew at my kitchen table and only came to the studio to rent kiln time.

It is the first time I have had one place for both halves of my work. The mornings are for clay, when the light is flat and cool and [[Ines]] is at the wheel next to me. The afternoons are for drawing, when the sun comes round to the long table and the paper dries faster. Most days I go home with clay under my nails and paint on my wrist.

So far this year has mostly taught me to wait, which I am bad at. It holds the [[First Firing]], the start of [[Picture Book]] and the chase for [[The Blue Glaze]].

## Key facts

- Started 2026-09-01, when I moved my desk in. It has no end yet.
- I pay half the rent and half the kiln electricity; Ines pays the other half and keeps the firing schedule.
- My routine is clay before lunch and drawing after it.
