# Picture Book

## About

A sixteen-spread picture book for children of three to six, about Mop, the cat of an old lighthouse keeper, who has to keep the lamp lit on the night the keeper is stuck across the bay in a storm. It is my first book, and I am both writing and illustrating it.

## What I do here

I write the text and draw every spread, first as small pencil thumbnails, then as full-size watercolour and pencil paintings. I plan to finish all sixteen thumbnails before I start any final colour, so that the whole book reads well from one page to the next before I commit paint to any one of them.
