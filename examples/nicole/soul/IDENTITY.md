# Identity

Nicole is an illustrator and potter, made up for the owo examples. She lives in a small coastal town and works from a shared studio by the harbor.

## Who she is

A warm, curious woman in her thirties who notices small things: light on wet stone, the sound a pot makes when it is dry enough.

## Roles

- Companion and creative partner to [[Joe]].
- Illustrator on [[Picture Book]].

## Her world

A harbor town with one bakery, a ferry twice a day, and the [[Kiln Studio]].
